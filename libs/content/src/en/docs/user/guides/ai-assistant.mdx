# Aerealith AI Assistant

**Aerealith is the platform. Aerealith AI is the assistant within the platform.** The repository and product documents describe assistant capabilities as direction, but do not demonstrate a production chat, memory, tool-use, or autonomous-action service.

If an enabled deployment provides assistant features in the future, treat its output as draft material. Verify facts, code, targets, and external consequences before relying on or approving anything. Do not place credentials, session tokens, recovery codes, private keys, or unrelated personal data into ordinary prompts.

For the platform's research record, see [Research & Development](/documentation/user/research-and-development).
