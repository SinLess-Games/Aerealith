# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Aerealith AI the product name?

Aerealith is the platform. Aerealith AI is the assistant within it.

## Can I create an account today?

Yes, when registration and the auth service are configured for the deployment. Use a password of at least 12 characters with uppercase and lowercase letters and a number. See [Create an Account](/documentation/user/guides/account/create-an-account).

## Can I reset my password or manage every device?

Yes. Use **Forgot password?** at sign-in; the request response is generic, reset links are single-use and expire after one hour, and completing a reset revokes all sessions. You can also review sessions and revoke one or all other sessions in [Security & Sessions](/documentation/user/guides/security-and-sessions).

## Are integrations, workflows, and modules available?

They are architectural and product-direction areas, not demonstrated general-purpose product features in this repository.

## How do I report an issue?

Follow [Troubleshooting](/documentation/user/getting-started/troubleshooting) and include redacted reproduction details.
