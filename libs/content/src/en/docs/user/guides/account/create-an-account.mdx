# Create an Account

Registration is available at `/sign-up` when the deployment enables it and configures the auth service.

Open **Create account** and provide a username, email address, and password. A display name is optional. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include uppercase and lowercase letters plus a number. The form may also require a bot-protection check.

Use an email address you can access. After successful registration, the interface directs unverified accounts to [Email Verification](/documentation/user/guides/account/email-verification).

Do not share passwords, verification links, or bot-protection tokens. If registration is disabled or a service is not configured, use the access path provided by the deployment owner rather than retrying or creating duplicate accounts.
