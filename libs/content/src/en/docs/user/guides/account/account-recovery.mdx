# Account Recovery

Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen, enter the account email, and submit the request. The confirmation is deliberately the same whether or not an account exists. This prevents the page from revealing account membership.

If the account is eligible and email delivery is configured, the deployment sends a reset link. The link contains a random, single-use token that expires after one hour; only its hash is stored by the service. Use the most recent link, choose a new password of at least 12 characters with uppercase and lowercase letters and a number, then sign in again. Completing a reset revokes all existing sessions.

Never disclose your password, reset link, recovery code, or active session to a person claiming to provide support. If you suspect compromise, reset the password and review [Security & Sessions](/documentation/user/guides/security-and-sessions).
