# Discord

The Discord integration can support community workflows when it is enabled and authorized for the intended server.

## Connection checklist

1. Sign into the correct Discord account.
2. Select the intended server.
3. Review requested bot or application permissions.
4. Restrict access to the channels required by the workflow.
5. Confirm who is allowed to invoke or administer the integration.

## Sending or publishing content

Before approving a message, verify the server, channel, mentions, links, and attachments. Treat public channels as public disclosure. Do not publish secrets, private support details, internal logs, or personal information without authorization.

## Community moderation

Automation should support—not replace—clear community rules and human moderation. Escalate harassment, threats, suspected compromise, and sensitive disputes to authorized moderators.

## Troubleshooting

If Discord is unavailable, confirm the connection is active and that the integration still has access to the target channel. Inspect Discord before retrying a timed-out send to avoid duplicate messages.
