# Privacy

Use only the information, connected accounts, and permissions necessary for the task. The current repository includes public policy pages; those pages, together with the deployment's applicable notices, are authoritative for privacy terms and requests.

Do not assume planned assistant, workflow, integration, or notification capabilities process data in a particular way. Their behavior must be established by the enabled deployment and applicable policy before use.

Remove secrets and unnecessary personal data from issue reports, prompts, and uploads. See [Safety & Privacy](/documentation/user/safety-and-privacy) and [Accessibility](/documentation/user/safety-and-privacy/accessibility).
