# Safety & Privacy

Use the minimum information and permissions required to complete a task. Aerealith can assist with analysis and action, but the user remains responsible for deciding what data may be shared and what results may be used.

## Sensitive information

Do not place passwords, API keys, private keys, session tokens, recovery codes, or authentication challenges in ordinary prompts. Redact unrelated personal, customer, medical, financial, and confidential business information.

## Connected services

Review requested permissions before connecting an integration. Confirm the external account and target every time a workflow could send, publish, delete, deploy, purchase, or modify records.

## Human review

Use qualified review for:

- legal, medical, financial, or regulated decisions
- security and privacy changes
- production operations
- decisions affecting a person's access, rights, employment, or safety
- destructive or irreversible actions

## Reporting concerns

Stop the workflow if you suspect unauthorized access, unintended disclosure, or an incorrect high-impact action. Preserve relevant non-secret evidence, notify the appropriate owner, and rotate exposed credentials through the system that issued them.

For general output review, follow [Reviewing Results](/documentation/user/getting-started/reviewing-results).
