# Decisions

Decision records explain why a durable choice was made, which alternatives were considered, and what consequences follow.

Use an [ADR](/documentation/developer/decisions/adrs) for architecture, a [DEC](/documentation/developer/decisions/decs) for broader engineering choices, and a [Deprecation Record](/documentation/developer/decisions/deprecations) when retiring supported behavior.

Records are append-only historical evidence. Supersede an old record with a new one rather than rewriting its original context.
