# Requests for Comments

An RFC includes summary, motivation, goals, non-goals, detailed proposal, alternatives, compatibility, security, operations, rollout, unresolved questions, and decision timeline.

Assign an owner and reviewers. Record material feedback and the final disposition: accepted, rejected, withdrawn, or superseded.

Acceptance authorizes the direction, not necessarily immediate implementation. Link follow-up work and decision records.
