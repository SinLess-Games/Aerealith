# Research & Development

R&D documents what was investigated, how evidence was gathered, and how findings influence decisions.

Each document should identify question, scope, method, evidence, findings, limitations, status, confidence, owners, dates, and related work. Separate observed results from interpretation and recommendation.

Use [Analyses](/documentation/developer/research-and-development/analyses/frontend-framework-analysis), [Experiments](/documentation/developer/research-and-development/experiments), [Benchmarks](/documentation/developer/research-and-development/benchmarks), [Prototypes](/documentation/developer/research-and-development/prototypes), and [Feasibility Studies](/documentation/developer/research-and-development/feasibility-studies) according to method.
