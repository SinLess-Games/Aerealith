# Feasibility Studies

A feasibility study evaluates whether a proposed capability can satisfy required constraints.

Define requirements, assumptions, dependencies, alternatives, cost, performance, security, compliance, operational burden, and unknowns. Use targeted experiments or prototypes for high-uncertainty claims.

Conclude with feasible, conditionally feasible, infeasible, or insufficient evidence. List conditions and evidence that would change the conclusion.
