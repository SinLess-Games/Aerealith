# Benchmarks

Benchmarks require a defined question, representative workload, controlled environment, warm-up, repeated samples, and reported variability.

Record hardware or runtime, versions, configuration, dataset, measurement method, and raw results. Compare like with like and include resource cost alongside latency or throughput.

A benchmark is evidence for its tested conditions, not a universal ranking. Re-run when dependencies, runtime, or workload changes materially.
