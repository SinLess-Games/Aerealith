# Coverage Requirements

Coverage percentages show executed code, not correctness.

Every change should cover new behavior, regressions, important branches, and expected failures. Higher-risk authentication, authorization, billing, destructive operations, migrations, and public contracts require explicit success and denial cases.

Do not add low-value assertions merely to raise a number. Explain intentional exclusions, and keep generated code out of meaningful coverage decisions.

CI thresholds are the minimum; reviewers may require more based on impact.
